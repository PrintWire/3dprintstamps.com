About 3D Print Stamps

Where heritage craft meets digital fabrication — 3D printed stamps, embossing dies, wax seals, and letterpress blocks.

OUR STORY

Old Craft, New Tools

3D Print Stamps covers the craft of impression — custom rubber stamps, embossing and debossing dies, wax seals, and letterpress blocks, made with resin, TPU, and direct-metal printing that rivals hand-engraving.

From a monogram wax seal to a luxury packaging die, we follow the materials, the machines, and the makers bringing fine-detail fabrication to a centuries-old craft.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover 3D printed stamps, seals, and dies with real craft depth — the material, the detail, and the impression it leaves.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative source for additive manufacturing in the stamp, seal, and embossing crafts — connecting makers to the suppliers who serve them.
